AI has been used in the following ways in this project:
- Altering the clockface image to remove the white background to fit in with the website's design.
  Asked the AI to "Remove the white background from around the clock"
- Fitting the images next to the coursework descriptions.
  The prompt use here was "Fit the image next to the coursework description with roughly a 60:40 ratio"

  It also added the same flex property to my coursework-image css, and altered the width property and added display block on my coursework-item img css.
